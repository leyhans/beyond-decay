The Lead … or the End of a Legend

What Audi's brand without rings in China shows about a name without technology

Addendum to: The Rental Software

In February, in “The Rental Software”, we wrote down what a German car now buys from others: chips, cloud, AI models, batteries. What remained were the body, the chassis and the brand, “and the hope that the customer still recognizes the difference between a BMW and a BYD by the way the door feels”. Audi has since tested that hope in China, under conditions that could hardly be cleaner.

I. The legend

Since 1971 Audi has advertised with “Vorsprung durch Technik”, a lead through technology, and for a long time the phrase was true. In 1980 came the quattro four-wheel drive, in 1989 the economical TDI diesel, in 1994 the aluminium body of the A8. Within the Volkswagen group, Audi was the brand where the new appeared first.

In China this became a status symbol. For years the A6 was the official car of civil servants, and until 2018 Audi was the first among the premium brands there. As late as 2022 Audi earned more than ever before, with an operating margin of 12.2 per cent.

II. Two badges on one car

In November 2024 Audi presented a new brand in China: AUDI, in capital letters and without the four rings. The first car, the E5 Sportback, arrived in September 2025. Technically it is an IM L6. IM is an electric brand of the Chinese group SAIC. Platform, motor, battery, electronics and software come from SAIC, the driver assistance from the Chinese supplier Momenta. Audi contributed the design and the chassis tuning.

The bet was this: for the same technology the Chinese will pay more if a German name is on it. At launch the E5 cost about 2,000 to 3,700 euros more than the IM L6. In January 2026 it found 420 buyers. In February Audi cut the price to the level of the IM L6.

From then on two cars stood side by side that differed only in the badge. Same technology, same price. From March to September 2026 the IM L6 sold about 8,700 times, the E5 about 4,800 times. In September the IM L6 sold more than five times as often. The German name no longer brought a premium. It cost half the buyers.

With the second model, the large SUV E7X, Audi tried a different approach: its own platform, developed together with SAIC, and a fifth cheaper than the comparable IM model. In June about 4,000 were sold, in September about 1,500. The direct competitor from Xiaomi sold almost eight times as many in the same month.

A McKinsey survey in China explains why. 76 per cent of car buyers say foreign premium brands are worth less in an electric car than in a combustion car. Among those under 24 it is 83 per cent. And that is exactly where the market is moving.

III. The bill

In the third quarter of 2026 the Audi group had revenue of 12.9 billion euros, 18.5 per cent less than a year earlier. The operating result was 29 million euros, after 468 million in the same quarter of the previous year. The Audi brand on its own made a loss. What keeps the group in the black is Lamborghini, which earned 564 million euros operating profit in nine months with just under 8,000 cars. A few thousand supercars carry a brand that sells more than a million cars a year.

In 2020, 43 per cent of all Audis went to China; in the first nine months of 2026 it was 31 per cent. Only 7.4 per cent of the Audis sold in China were fully electric. The group’s own platform for the next generation, Volkswagen’s SSP, was due in 2026. It has been postponed to 2029, and Volkswagen is developing its electronics and software with Rivian. What that partnership has produced so far we described in “Two Patients in One Bed”.

Until then Audi has to earn money with today’s technology. By 2029 up to 7,500 jobs will go in Germany. In Neckarsulm the A8 has ended production, the car whose aluminium body embodied the lead in 1994. There is no firm successor. How Audi is at the same time planning a four-billion-dollar plant in Georgia is told in “Five Months Later”.

IV. The staircase

The newsletter “Der Autopreneur” describes the path Audi has taken in China as a staircase leading down. On the first steps a manufacturer buys individual technologies or develops them with partners. Everyone does that. On the third it uses the partner’s platform. On the fourth it takes the partner’s car and puts its own badge on it. On the last ones it only licenses its name or sells the brand altogether, as happened with Volvo and MG.

The E5 stands on the fourth step. The four AUDI models due from 2028 are being developed in a joint company in which SAIC holds more shares than Audi.

So the chain of dependency from “The Rental Software” is not a list of suppliers. It is a staircase, and one goes down it step by step. Each step is reasonable in itself, because the partner is faster and one’s own house does not deliver. In the end all one owns is the badge.

V. What must be said in fairness

In Europe Audi is selling more cars than a year ago. In a YouGov survey Audi is the most popular car brand in Germany, just ahead of BMW and Mercedes. In January 2026 the E5 was voted car of the year in China, and European motoring magazines praise it. Audi’s chief executive Gernot Döllner calls building the new brand a marathon, not a sprint.

All of this is true. It does not change the fact that the same car at the same price was bought twice as often without the name Audi.

VI. The end of a legend

A brand is stored achievement. It tells the buyer: what these people built in the past was good, so this will be good too. That is a credit, and like every credit it has to be serviced. For forty years Audi serviced it with mechanical engineering: four-wheel drive, engine, body. Today technology in a car means software, AI and battery, and these come from SAIC, Momenta and Rivian.

In “Excellent Production Engineering” we wrote that in Germany production engineering is now only what we live on. Audi shows what happens when even that is no longer enough. The Chinese do not recognize the difference between an Audi and an IM by the door. They recognize it by the badge, and the badge now stands for good cars of yesterday.

The lead has been used up. What remains is the legend. And in China it sells worse than the same car without a name.
