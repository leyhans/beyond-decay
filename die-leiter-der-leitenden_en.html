The Conductors of the Leaders

Who leads the leaders?

A conductor generates no current. It carries it, it decides what gets through, and without it the voltage has no effect. The same holds for the ideologies and mentors behind those in power: they do not design the policy, but they supply the language in which it can be presented as necessary — and they bind whoever speaks it.

The series proceeds case by case. Each text stands on its own; together they make a book. The typology comes at the end, not at the beginning, so that the cases do not have to fit a scheme.
