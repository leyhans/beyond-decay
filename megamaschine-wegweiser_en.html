Ten questions to a five-thousand-year-old apparatus. Three texts to start with for each, everything else behind them.

    1. Lewis Mumford · The Myth of the MachineLewis Mumford found it at the building of the pyramids: people as parts of a machine.
    2. Fabian Scheidler · The End of the MegamachineFive hundred years of state, market, military and ideology as a single apparatus.
    3. The Genesis of the MegamachineIt did not descend on us. People built it, one decision at a time.
